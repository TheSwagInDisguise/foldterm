# One home screen for work and play

Everything you check twenty times a day sits in a pane one tap away, each reading straight from your phone.

⌂Home your way

Pick and arrange cards: happening now, agenda, summary, widgets, quick dial, inbox, music, weather, tasks, notes, clocks, quick actions.

!Inbox without the apps

Mail, chats and alerts from your notifications, with reply, archive and mark-as-read buttons, snooze, pin and mute.

▦Calendar & tasks

Agenda, day timeline, week grid and month, quick-add in plain English, and tasks with due days and stars.

♫Media

A full player for whatever's playing, with seek, ±30 s jumps, app switching, output and volume.

☁Weather

Hourly rain, 7-day range bars, UV, wind, sunrise and sunset, warnings, and saved places. No API key needed.

◎Focus & screen time

Focus sessions hide distracting apps and silence the phone; screen time shows where the hours went.

◐Controls

Ringer, Do Not Disturb, volumes, brightness, rotation, timeout and torch, without pulling down the shade.

◧Split screen & pairs

Open two apps side by side from a long-press or split a | b, and save pairs you use daily.

◫Widgets & icon packs

Host any Android widget, use any standard icon pack or tinted mono icons, and choose from five themes.

☺People

Photos, groups, emergency contacts, birthdays, quick call, text, email and WhatsApp.

✎Notes & news

Autosaving notes with tappable checklists, and RSS news with unread tracking and a saved list.

⚙Make it yours

Custom and day/night themes, text size, reduced motion, gestures, folders, renames, backup and restore.

# The prompt

Tap the search bar, press / or tap 🎤. Results appear as you type, and anything that isn't a command becomes a web search.

❯ open spotify                 # or just type its name
❯ call mum · sms maya running late · wa sam on my way
❯ event fri 3pm dentist        # a filled-in event
❯ todo add pay rent fri !       # due Friday, starred
❯ 18% of 240 · 5 km in mi · 100 usd in aud · time tokyo
❯ timer 5m tea · alarm 7:30 · focus 25m
❯ split chrome | notes · pair work
❯ dnd on; vol 20; bright 40     # chain with ;
❯ alias gm=dnd off; brief
❯ neofetch · weather paris · notifs clear · !!

# Install

  1. Download foldterm-x.y.z.apk from the latest release and open it on your phone.
  2. Open FoldTerm and accept the permission prompts (every one is optional).
  3. Settings → home app → set default.
  4. Settings → notifs → grant to turn on Mail, Chat, Alerts and Media.
The phone says the app "requests access to sensitive data"

Google Play Protect flags sideloaded apps that can read notifications. Install the -lite APK, which leaves that out, or briefly turn off Play Protect scanning while you install the full APK.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. There's no account, analytics or backend. FoldTerm only contacts Open-Meteo for weather, open.er-api.com when you convert currency, and the news feeds you choose.

Does it work on phones that don't fold?

Yes. Narrow screens get the cover layout and wide ones the two-pane layout; the fold-specific touches simply switch off.
